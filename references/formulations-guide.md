# Formulation Bank: Precise vs. Imprecise

This reference contains examples only. Rules and evaluation criteria are in `formulations-commentary.md`.

---

## 1. Research Problem

### 1.1. Substituting the Topic for the Problem

❌ "The research problem is the digital transformation of Russian enterprises."

✅ "Russian enterprises are actively implementing digital technologies, yet there is no empirically grounded methodology for assessing organizational readiness for digital transformation, which leads to a high proportion of unsuccessful projects (up to 70%, according to McKinsey, 2023)."

---

❌ "The problem of this study is the use of artificial intelligence in medicine."

✅ "AI-based clinical decision support systems are being adopted in practice; however, the discrepancy between diagnostic model accuracy under laboratory conditions (AUC > 0.95) and real clinical settings (AUC 0.70–0.80) has no systematic explanation, which undermines practicing physicians' trust in the system's recommendations."

---

❌ "The problem is the development of ESG practices in the Russian corporate sector."

✅ "Russian companies publish ESG reports according to international standards (GRI, SASB); however, the correlation between stated ESG indicators and actual environmental and social outcomes has not been verified on Russian data, calling into question the informativeness of these disclosures for investors."

### 1.2. Problem Is Too Broad

❌ "The problem is the insufficient study of artificial intelligence's impact on the economy."

✅ "Existing models for assessing the economic impact of generative AI adoption in the professional services sector do not account for the decline in quality of expert judgment under excessive automation, leading to overstated ROI projections."

---

❌ "The problem is ineffective public administration."

✅ "Interdepartmental electronic interaction via SMEV covers more than 200 federal services; however, the average processing time for an interdepartmental request (4.2 days, according to the Ministry of Digital Development, 2024) is three times the regulatory norm, and the causes have not been systematized."

---

❌ "The problem is the insufficient development of financial technologies."

✅ "Mobile banking penetration in Russia has reached 82% (Bank of Russia, 2024); however, conversion from app installation to active use of investment products is under 7%, while the factors determining this gap have been studied only in mature markets (USA, UK) and have not been verified under conditions of high inflation and currency restrictions."

### 1.3. Problem Is a Platitude

❌ "In current conditions, the problem of improving personnel management effectiveness is relevant."

✅ "The increase in the share of remote employees in IT companies from 15% to 60% between 2020 and 2023 demonstrated that traditional KPI systems cannot evaluate the productivity of distributed teams; meanwhile, alternative metrics (OKR, results-oriented frameworks) lack sufficient empirical validation in the Russian context."

---

❌ "The problem consists of the need to improve the educational process."

✅ "The transition to a hybrid learning format in universities revealed a gap: online-stream students score 18% lower on final assessments compared to in-person students (HSE University monitoring data, 2023); however, whether the gap stems from motivational, technological, or pedagogical factors has not been differentiated."

---

❌ "The problem of software import substitution remains relevant."

✅ "By 2025, 78% of government agencies had migrated to domestic operating systems (Ministry of Digital Development registry); however, compatibility of domestic software with legacy document management systems is ensured in fewer than 40% of cases, blocking a complete transition away from foreign solutions; a systematic comparison of migration strategies (parallel launch vs. full replacement) in the Russian public sector has not been conducted."

### 1.4. No Research Gap

❌ "The problem of digitalization of education has been studied by Ivanov (2020), Petrova (2021), and Sidorov (2022). The topic continues to attract researchers' attention."

✅ "Ivanov (2020) and Petrova (2021) studied student motivation in online courses; Sidorov (2022) studied infrastructural barriers. However, none of these papers examines the interaction between these factors: how infrastructural constraints moderate the effect of motivational interventions on learning outcomes."

---

❌ "The literature contains extensive coverage of risk management in construction."

✅ "Existing risk management models in construction (PMI, PRINCE2) were developed for project cycles of 12–36 months. Infrastructure construction projects with cycles of 5–10 years and phased budget financing have a distinct risk structure not described in any of the 12 models analyzed in the meta-review by Chen et al. (2023)."

## 2. Relevance

### 2.1. Empty Formulas

❌ "In today's world, this topic is gaining particular relevance..."

❌ "At present, the problem of... is one of the most significant..."

❌ "Relevance is driven by the need for..." (without specifying whose need and why)

❌ "The relevance of the topic is beyond doubt, because..."

❌ "In the context of globalization and digitalization, the topic takes on new meaning."

✅ "The volume of the Russian EdTech market grew from 50 to 110 billion rubles between 2021 and 2024 (Smart Ranking, 2024). The Digital Transformation of Education Strategy to 2030 identifies adaptive educational technologies as a priority. Yet an analysis of 47 publications from 2019–2024 in the Scopus database shows that only 3 papers examine adaptive learning in the context of Russian universities."

### 2.2. Practical Relevance Only, Without Scientific

❌ "Companies are actively implementing blockchain, so the topic is relevant."

✅ "Despite the growth in corporate blockchain projects (Gartner, 2024), a systematic analysis of factors determining the success of transitioning from pilot to production deployment is absent from the scientific literature."

---

❌ "The government is allocating significant funds for AI development, which makes the topic relevant."

✅ "The federal project 'Artificial Intelligence' provides funding of 90 billion rubles through 2030; however, methodologies for assessing the socioeconomic effects of public AI investment have not been developed — existing models (Accenture, 2023; PwC, 2024) are based on private-sector data and do not account for the specifics of budget financing and government procurement."

### 2.3. Scientific Relevance Only, Without Practical

❌ "The theoretical literature has insufficiently studied question X, which determines the scientific relevance of this paper."

✅ "The theoretical gap — the absence of a model for assessing X — has a direct practical consequence: companies make investment decisions based on expert estimates with a spread of ±40% (Industry Report, 2024), whereas a formalized model would reduce uncertainty to ±15%."

### 2.4. Relevance Based on Outdated Data

❌ "According to World Bank data (2015), digitalization contributes to economic growth."

✅ "According to World Bank data (2024), the contribution of the digital economy to GDP in developing countries grew from 4.5% (2018) to 7.2% (2023); Russia trails the BRICS median by 1.8 percentage points."

## 3. State of Research on the Problem

### 3.1. Listing Authors Without Systematization

❌ "Problem X has been studied by A.A. Ivanov, B.B. Petrov, V.V. Sidorov, G.G. Kozlov, D.D. Novikov, E.E. Fedorov, Zh.Zh. Mikhailov."

✅ "The theoretical foundations of X were developed in the works of A.A. Ivanov and B.B. Petrov (structural approach) and V.V. Sidorov (process approach). Empirical verification was conducted by G.G. Kozlov on industrial enterprise data and by D.D. Novikov on service-sector data. Institutional aspects of the problem are examined in the works of E.E. Fedorov."

### 3.2. No Conclusion About the Gap After the Review

❌ "Thus, the research topic is well represented in the scientific literature."

✅ "Thus, existing studies focus on large manufacturing enterprises. The small and medium-sized service sector remains unstudied, despite accounting for 52% of employment (Rosstat, 2024)."

---

❌ "Many aspects of the problem remain understudied."

✅ "None of the reviewed works examines the effect of factor Z on the relationship between X and Y, although pilot study data (Author, 2023) suggest it may play a moderating role."

### 3.3. Mixing Schools Without Comparing Approaches

❌ "The topic has been studied by both domestic researchers (Ivanov, Petrov) and international ones (Smith, Chen)."

✅ "International literature is dominated by quantitative approaches with large samples (Smith, 2021: N=500; Chen, 2022: N=1200). Domestic papers (Ivanov, 2020; Petrov, 2022) use qualitative methods with small samples (N=15–30), which limits generalizability but provides depth of analysis of Russian-specific factors."

---

## 4. Research Goal

### 4.1. Process-Goal Instead of Result-Goal

❌ "The goal is to study the impact of digitalization on project management."

✅ "The goal is to develop a model for assessing the impact of digital tools on key project management performance metrics in Russian IT companies."

---

❌ "The goal is to examine the motivational characteristics of Generation Z."

✅ "The goal is to identify and rank work motivation factors among Generation Z representatives in Russian IT companies to justify adjustments to HR policies."

---

❌ "The goal of the paper is to examine cryptocurrency regulation mechanisms."

✅ "The goal is to identify the optimal regulatory model for crypto-asset circulation in the Russian Federation through a comparative analysis of regulatory regimes in 5 jurisdictions across 12 criteria."

### 4.2. Goal Is a List Separated by Commas

❌ "The goal is to analyze, systematize, and develop recommendations for..."

✅ "The goal is to develop recommendations for optimizing X based on an analysis of Y."

---

❌ "The goal is to identify trends, determine factors, and propose measures to improve..."

✅ "The goal is to justify a set of measures for improving X based on a factor analysis of Y."

### 4.3. Goal Is Not Measurable

❌ "The goal is to reveal the essence of digital transformation."

✅ "The goal is to identify and classify key barriers to digital transformation in industrial enterprises based on an analysis of 30 cases from 2020–2024."

---

❌ "The goal is to deepen understanding of the problem of sustainable development."

✅ "The goal is to assess the impact of ESG ratings on the cost of debt capital for Russian companies based on a sample of 120 issuers over 2019–2024."

---

❌ "The goal is to illuminate the issues of AI adoption in public administration."

✅ "The goal is to develop a prioritization matrix for government services suitable for AI implementation, based on criteria of labor intensity, frequency, and standardizability."

### 4.4. Mismatch Between Goal and Problem

❌ Problem: "absence of a methodology for assessing X"
   Goal: "analyze international experience in assessing X"

✅ Problem: "absence of a methodology for assessing X"
   Goal: "develop a methodology for assessing X, adapted to conditions Y"

---

❌ Problem: "contradiction between A and B"
   Goal: "study the theoretical foundations of A"

✅ Problem: "contradiction between A and B"
   Goal: "identify conditions under which A and B are reconcilable, and develop a mechanism for their integration"

### 4.5. Vague Verbs

❌ "study", "consider", "examine", "reveal", "illuminate"

✅ "develop" (model, methodology, algorithm, recommendations)
✅ "identify" (factors, patterns, dependencies)
✅ "assess" (effectiveness, impact, degree)
✅ "determine" (criteria, conditions, parameters)
✅ "justify" (approach, choice, strategy)
✅ "verify" (model, hypothesis, dependency)
✅ "classify" (types, groups, categories)

## 5. Research Objectives

### 5.1. Irrelevant Objective

Goal: "develop a model for assessing the effectiveness of remote work"

❌ "Examine the history of remote work development."

✅ "Systematize existing metrics for evaluating work effectiveness as applied to the remote format."

---

Goal: "justify a market entry strategy for company X in market Y"

❌ "Analyze the global macroeconomic situation."

✅ "Assess the capacity and competitive structure of market Y using Porter's five forces model."

### 5.2. Objectives Do Not Cover the Goal

Goal: "develop a methodology for assessing X"

❌ (1) analyze theory, (2) conduct a survey, (3) formulate conclusions.

✅ (1) systematize existing approaches to assessing X, (2) conduct empirical research on X factors on sample N, (3) develop a system of assessment criteria for X based on results, (4) validate the methodology on a control sample.

---

Goal: "identify factors influencing Y"

❌ (1) study theoretical aspects, (2) conduct analysis, (3) draw conclusions.

✅ (1) identify potential factors of Y based on a literature review, (2) operationalize variables and form the sample, (3) conduct correlation and regression analysis of the data, (4) interpret results and compare with theoretical premises.

### 5.3. Objective = Retelling of a Chapter

❌ "Examine the theoretical foundations of the problem."

✅ "Determine the theoretical framework for analyzing X based on a comparison of approaches A, B, and C."

---

❌ "Conduct an analysis of practical experience."

✅ "Conduct a comparative analysis of 5 implementation cases of X against criteria Y1, Y2, Y3."

---

❌ "Develop practical recommendations."

✅ "Formulate recommendations for adapting methodology X for companies of type Y with constraints Z."

### 5.4. Vague Verbs in Objectives

❌ "Consider the main approaches to..."
❌ "Study the characteristics of..."
❌ "Reveal the content of the concept..."
❌ "Examine the problem..."

✅ "Systematize approaches to X by criteria Y"
✅ "Identify differences in Z between groups A and B"
✅ "Operationalize concept X through indicators Y1, Y2, Y3"
✅ "Assess the statistical significance of the relationship between X and Y"

### 5.5. Objectives Violate Logical Sequence

❌ (1) Develop recommendations, (2) analyze theory, (3) conduct a survey.

✅ (1) Analyze theoretical approaches to X, (2) conduct empirical research on Y, (3) develop recommendations based on results from (1) and (2).

## 6. Hypothesis

### 6.1. Truism Hypothesis

❌ "The use of digital technologies improves management effectiveness."

✅ "Implementing predictive analytics tools in project management reduces schedule deviation by 15–25% in projects with team sizes exceeding 20 people, but has no significant effect with smaller teams."

---

❌ "Employee training positively affects labor productivity."

✅ "Upskilling programs lasting more than 40 hours produce a statistically significant productivity gain (β > 0.15, p < 0.05) only when a post-training support system is in place (mentoring, OJT); without it, the training effect diminishes within 3 months."

### 6.2. Non-Falsifiable Hypothesis

❌ "Digital transformation is an important factor in economic development."

✅ "Companies that have completed a full digital transformation cycle (per the MIT CISR model) demonstrate EBITDA margin growth of ≥5 percentage points compared to same-sector companies that have not undergone transformation, over a 3-year period following completion."

---

❌ "Quality education contributes to career advancement."

✅ "Graduates of top-10 Russian MBA programs (Forbes ranking, 2024) reach C-level positions on average 3 years earlier than graduates of programs outside the top 30, controlling for initial position level and industry."

### 6.3. Hypothesis as Disguised Goal

❌ "It is assumed that the developed methodology will be effective."

✅ "The developed methodology will forecast X with an accuracy (R²) of no less than 0.7 on the validation sample."

---

❌ "It is assumed that the proposed recommendations will improve the situation."

✅ "Implementation of the proposed KPI system will lead to a reduction in staff turnover of ≥10% within 12 months compared to a control group of divisions."

### 6.4. Hypothesis Without Variables

❌ "Risk management affects project outcomes."

✅ "Projects in which a formalized risk management procedure (identification, assessment, response per PMBOK) is applied from the first phase have budget deviation of ≤15%, while projects using an ad hoc approach show deviation of ≥30% (on a sample of ≥50 projects)."

---

❌ "Corporate culture is related to a company's innovativeness."

✅ "Companies with a dominant adhocratic culture type (per the Cameron-Quinn model) file 40% more patent applications per 1,000 employees than companies with a dominant hierarchical culture, controlling for industry and size."

## 7. Object and Subject

### 7.1. Object = Subject

❌ Object: "digital transformation of enterprises"
   Subject: "the process of digital transformation of enterprises"

✅ Object: "industrial enterprises implementing digital transformation programs"
   Subject: "organizational barriers to the transition from pilot projects to large-scale digital technology deployment"

---

❌ Object: "the cryptocurrency market"
   Subject: "the functioning of the cryptocurrency market"

✅ Object: "the cryptocurrency market of the Russian Federation"
   Subject: "regulatory influence mechanisms on crypto-asset circulation and their effect on the volume of legal transactions"

---

❌ Object: "marketing in social networks"
   Subject: "the use of marketing in social networks"

✅ Object: "Russian D2C clothing brands using social networks as the primary sales channel"
   Subject: "the effect of content format (UGC, influencer collaborations, promo) on conversion to purchase"

### 7.2. Subject Not Aligned With Goal

Goal: "develop a risk assessment model"

❌ Subject: "the organizational culture of the company"

✅ Subject: "risk factors and their interrelationships in digital transformation projects"

---

Goal: "assess the effectiveness of loyalty programs"

❌ Subject: "the customer relationship management system"

✅ Subject: "the effect of loyalty program type (points-based, cashback, status-based) on repeat purchase frequency and customer LTV"

### 7.3. Object Too Broad or Too Narrow

❌ Object: "the economy of Russia" (when studying a single sector)

✅ Object: "oil and gas sector enterprises in the Russian Federation with revenues exceeding 10 billion rubles"

---

❌ Object: "LLC 'Romashka'" (when claiming generalizable conclusions)

✅ Object: "small retail trade enterprises in the Moscow region (using LLC 'Romashka' and similar companies as examples)"

---

## 8. Scientific Novelty

### 8.1. Declarative Novelty

❌ "A comprehensive analysis of problem X has been conducted for the first time."

✅ "A typology of digital transformation barriers has been proposed comprising three levels (technological, organizational, institutional), which supplements existing single-level classifications (Author1, Author2) and enables the accounting of cross-level interactions."

---

❌ "Approaches to... have been systematized for the first time."

✅ "A comparative matrix of 8 approaches to assessing X across 5 criteria (Y1–Y5) has been constructed, allowing researchers to make a justified method selection based on sample characteristics."

---

❌ "This problem has been studied for the first time using Russian companies as examples."

✅ "The model of Author (year) has been verified on Russian data (N=150, 2020–2024): relationships A→B and C→D were confirmed; relationship E→F was not confirmed, indicating the institutional conditioning of factor E."

### 8.2. Novelty Overstated for the Level of Work

❌ "A fundamentally new theory of innovation management has been developed." (master's thesis)

✅ "Model X (Author, year) has been extended through the inclusion of factor Y, which raises the model's explanatory power from R²=0.45 to R²=0.62 on dataset Z."

---

❌ "A universal methodology for assessing X has been created." (PhD dissertation)

✅ "A methodology for assessing X for enterprises of type Y has been developed, validated on a sample of N=30 organizations; the scope of applicability is limited to conditions Z."

### 8.3. Novelty Does Not Distinguish From Existing Work

❌ "An analysis of factors influencing X has been conducted." (when such analysis already exists)

✅ "Unlike the studies of Author1 (quantitative approach, N=200) and Author2 (case study, N=5), this paper employs a mixed methods design (quantitative analysis N=150 + 20 in-depth interviews), enabling an explanation of the mechanism behind the identified dependencies."

---

## 9. Theoretical Significance

### 9.1. Substituting Significance With a Retelling of Content

❌ "The theoretical significance consists of a systematization of the literature on topic X."

✅ "The theoretical significance lies in the extension of the model of Author (year) through the inclusion of variable Y, which explains an additional 15% of the variance in the dependent variable not explained by the original model."

---

❌ "The results contribute to the development of management theory."

✅ "The results demonstrate the limited applicability of theory X under conditions Y (hypotheses H1 and H2 confirmed; H3 rejected), clarifying the boundary conditions of the theory and opening a direction for studying alternative explanatory mechanisms."

### 9.2. Significance Not Connected to the Paper's Results

❌ "The theoretical significance is determined by the importance of the problem for science."

✅ "The developed classification enables the operationalization of concept X, which was previously used in the literature without a unified definition (7 distinct interpretations identified across 23 sources)."

---

## 10. Practical Significance

### 10.1. No Recipient and No Application Context

❌ "The results can be used in practice."

✅ "The developed risk assessment algorithm can be used by project management offices in IT companies with a portfolio of 10 or more projects to prioritize risks at the initiation stage; the algorithm was piloted in company X and reduced risk assessment time from 5 to 2 working days."

---

❌ "The practical significance is driven by the possibility of applying the results in enterprise activities."

✅ "The proposed calculation methodology can be applied by regional offices of the Federal Tax Service when verifying the validity of transfer prices in crypto-asset transactions; the methodology integrates into the existing desk audit procedure without additional IT infrastructure."

### 10.2. Overstated Practical Significance

❌ "The research results will be beneficial to all companies in Russia."

✅ "The results are applicable to manufacturing companies with 100–500 employees; scaling to large holding structures requires adaptation of module X."

---

## 11. Propositions Submitted for Defense

### 11.1. Proposition Is a Platitude

❌ "Digital transformation has a significant impact on organizational activities."

✅ "The level of digital maturity of an organization (measured on a 5-component scale) is a significant predictor of operational effectiveness (β=0.42, p<0.001); organization size plays a moderating role: the effect is significant only for companies with more than 200 employees."

### 11.2. Proposition Is a Retelling of an Objective or Method

❌ "A comparative analysis of approaches to assessing X has been conducted."

✅ "It has been established that approach A outperforms approaches B and C by the criterion of forecast accuracy (MAPE: A=12%, B=23%, C=31%) on Russian enterprise data, while differences on international data are not statistically significant."

### 11.3. Proposition Contains No Author's Own Result

❌ "It has been established that motivation affects performance (per Herzberg's theory)."

✅ "It has been established that in Russian IT companies, Herzberg's hygiene factors (working conditions, pay level) act as motivators for Junior specialists (tenure < 2 years), which contradicts the classical model and is explained by the high mobility of this labor market segment."

---

## 12. Conclusions and Conclusion Chapter

### 12.1. Conclusion Is a Platitude

❌ "It has been established that digital transformation has a significant impact on company effectiveness."

✅ "Regression analysis of data from 84 companies showed that the level of digital maturity (per the IDC scale) explains 38% of the variance in operational effectiveness (β=0.42, p<0.001), with the greatest effect observed in companies with revenues exceeding 5 billion rubles."

### 12.2. Generalization Beyond the Data

❌ "The results confirm that all Russian companies need to..." (based on a sample of 12 Moscow IT companies)

✅ "The results indicate a trend [X] in the segment of mid-cap Moscow IT companies. Generalization to other industries and regions requires additional verification."

---

❌ "It has been proven that method X is the optimal way to solve problem Y."

✅ "On the studied sample, method X demonstrated the best results among the three compared methods (X, Y, Z) by criterion K. Extension of the conclusion to the general population is limited by the non-representative nature of the sample (convenience sample, N=45)."

### 12.3. Conclusions Do Not Correspond to Objectives

❌ Objective: "Identify factors X"
   Conclusion: "The question of factors X is relevant and requires further study."

✅ Objective: "Identify factors X"
   Conclusion: "Three groups of factors X have been identified: organizational (F1, F2), technological (F3), and regulatory (F4). F2 has the greatest explanatory power (β=0.38, p<0.01)."

### 12.4. Conclusion Chapter Is a Retelling of Chapters

❌ "Chapter One examined the theoretical aspects... Chapter Two conducted an analysis... Chapter Three developed..."

✅ "The study demonstrated that [key result 1]. This confirms / refutes / refines [theoretical position]. The practical implication is [recommendation]. A limitation of the study is [X], which defines the direction for further research: [Y]."

### 12.5. No Directions for Further Research

❌ "The topic requires further study."

✅ "Further research should be directed toward: (1) verifying the model on a sample of small business enterprises (the current sample is limited to medium and large companies); (2) incorporating variable X into the model, which was not available within the scope of this study."

## 13. Definitions and Terminology

### 13.1. Tautological Definition

❌ "In this paper, digital transformation is understood as the digital transformation of an enterprise."

✅ "In this paper, digital transformation is understood as the systemic change of the business model, operational processes, and organizational culture of an enterprise through the adoption of digital technologies (in accordance with the definition of Westerman et al., 2014)."

### 13.2. Contradictory Use of a Term

❌ In section 1.1: "effectiveness" = financial indicators.
   In section 2.3: "effectiveness" = customer satisfaction.

✅ In the "Conceptual Framework" section: "Operational effectiveness (OE) — the ratio of actual output to planned output, adjusted for quality (defect rate ≤ 2%). Throughout all sections of this paper, the term 'effectiveness' is used in this sense unless otherwise specified."

### 13.3. Author's Definition Without Justification

❌ "We define X as process Y." (without explaining why)

✅ "The literature identifies three approaches to defining X: as a process (Author1), as an outcome (Author2), and as a state (Author3). In this study, X is defined as a process, since the object of analysis is the dynamics of change, not a fixed state."

## 14. Argumentation and Logical Errors

### 14.1. Appeal to Authority Without Analysis

❌ "As Porter argues, competitiveness is determined by five forces; therefore, company X must..."

✅ "Porter's five forces model is applicable under conditions of stable industry structure. In sector Y, industry boundaries are blurred due to platform business models, which limits the model's applicability. Nevertheless, the forces of 'threat of substitutes' and 'bargaining power of buyers' remain relevant, justifying the model's partial use."

### 14.2. Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc

❌ "After system X was implemented, profit grew by 20%; therefore, system X drove the profit growth."

✅ "After system X was implemented, profit grew by 20%. However, during the same period, the market grew by 15% and raw material prices declined. To isolate the effect of system X, a difference-in-differences method was applied, using a control group of 10 comparable enterprises that did not implement the system."

### 14.3. False Dichotomy

❌ "A company can either invest in innovation or maintain stability."

✅ "The 'ambidexterity' strategy (O'Reilly & Tushman, 2016) involves simultaneous exploitation and exploration activities through organizational separation. The choice between innovation and stability is not binary."

### 14.4. Circular Reasoning

❌ "The system is effective because it yields good results. The results are good because the system is effective."

✅ "System effectiveness was assessed by three independent criteria: (1) request processing time (reduced by 30%), (2) error rate (reduced from 8% to 2%), (3) user satisfaction (NPS increased from 35 to 62). Data were collected before and after implementation on the same user sample (N=200)."

### 14.5. Substituting Correlation for Causation

❌ "A correlation between X and Y was found (r=0.65); therefore, X causes Y."

✅ "A correlation between X and Y was found (r=0.65, p<0.01). A causal interpretation is limited by the cross-sectional study design. To test the direction of the relationship, a longitudinal design or instrumental variables are required."

## 15. Formulations of the Methodological Apparatus in the Introduction

### 15.1. Theoretical Framework — Enumeration Without Connection to the Study

❌ "The theoretical framework consists of the works of Ivanov, Petrov, Sidorov, Porter, Kotler, Drucker..."

✅ "The theoretical framework consists of: the concept of digital maturity (Westerman et al., 2014), which underpins the assessment scale; Kotter's theory of organizational change (1996), which defined the structure of barrier analysis; and the TAM model (Davis, 1989), used to measure user technology acceptance."

### 15.2. Structure of the Work — Formal Enumeration

❌ "The paper consists of an introduction, three chapters, a conclusion, a list of references, and appendices."

✅ "Chapter One establishes the theoretical framework for the study and justifies the choice of analytical model. Chapter Two describes the methodology, sample, and data collection procedure. Chapter Three presents the results of the empirical analysis and their interpretation. The conclusion presents the main findings and limitations of the study."

### 15.3. Methods — Enumeration Without Justification

❌ "The paper uses analysis, synthesis, induction, deduction, comparison, and generalization."

✅ "Comparative analysis across a matrix of 5 criteria was used to systematize approaches to X. Semi-structured interviews (N=20) with subsequent thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006) were used to identify factors Y. Multiple regression on data from N=150 enterprises was used for quantitative verification of dependencies."

---

❌ "Methods: systems approach, structural analysis, expert assessments."

✅ "The systems approach was used to build a model of factor interrelationships for X (section 2.1). Structural analysis was applied in decomposing process Y into 4 stages (section 2.2). Expert assessments (Delphi method, 3 rounds, 12 experts) were used to validate the weight coefficients of the model (section 3.3)."
